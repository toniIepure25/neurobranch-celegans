# Minimal decisive experiment, v2 draft

Prepared 25 November 2026. This is an execution design for approval. Instrument calibration used the finite synthetic fixtures in the [estimand specification](IC1_V2_ESTIMAND_SPEC.md). No models were fit and no data or outcomes were generated for this proposed experiment.

## Question and fixed information contract

When can neural observation history predict a specified intervention response, and how much error comes from structural ambiguity, finite samples and compression? Does one extra nonperturbing measurement recover the missing information for that response?

| Item | Design |
|---|---|
| Two mechanisms | The three-position ring with latent velocity ±1 and persistence 0.85. Each episode draws context C independently with probabilities 0.5 and 0.5. Conditional generators M₀ and M₁ keep C fixed within the episode. Initialize position and velocity uniformly. Passive dynamics change velocity and then move modulo three. Neither passive transitions nor emissions use C. |
| Base observations | The last two consecutive noiseless positions H = (Pₜ₋₁,Pₜ), with the passive action known. No earlier intervention outcome, context identity, shared subject label or mechanism-linked auxiliary data is available for an evaluation episode. |
| Single intervention and response | Apply the asymmetric probe once after H. Draw velocity according to the same transition and shift position by that velocity plus C modulo three. The terminal response Y is the next physical position on the common three-category alphabet. The response is measured once. |
| One added measurement | A perfect synthetic context sensor S=C read at the checkpoint before the probe. It has zero misclassification probability and changes no transition or outcome. It costs one read per episode and is accessible during both training and inference in the sensor arm. Base-arm features are obtained by masking S on those same episodes. |
| Compression | Replace H by Z=Pₜ. Pass S through unchanged in the sensor arm. No test outcomes or fit-dependent labels enter this choice. |

The observable passive laws agree at all lengths by equality of the position/velocity kernels and emissions in the two contexts. The two-position H determines velocity since the two nonzero moves are distinct modulo three. At each H, context-specific probe laws are TV 0.85 apart. The triangle inequality gives a worst-case lower bound of 0.425 for any allowed predictor. The midpoint attains that bound and also attains Bayes risk 0.425 in this balanced assay. The perfect S eliminates this two-law ambiguity given H. Given Z and S, velocity remains symmetric and the two possible response rows are TV 0.70 apart. The compressed sensor-arm oracle risk is 0.35. Conditional test labels and exact model parameters are evaluator-only. [Independently verified rows](IC1_V2_ACCEPTANCE_RESULTS.json) · [Prior-art delta](MINIMAL_EXPERIMENT_PRIOR_ART_DELTA.md)

## Estimands and fair baseline

Evaluate a predicted categorical distribution Q against the known response distribution conditional on the actual microscopic start, using total variation. Average over independent evaluation episodes and record separate context risks as a diagnostic. This choice makes a claim about the hidden conditional response rather than about predicting a noisy categorical realization or the population mixture.

- **Structural ambiguity.** An exact belief state uses the known model class and prior but only the permitted inference input. Compute its minimal TV risk R*(H) or R*(H,S). Resolve Bayes ties by using a midpoint for two equally weighted laws. Where more than two laws remain after compression, compute the Bayes action by minimizing the weighted L1 distance over the simplex. In all cases compute the identification region separately from its loss-dependent risk.
- **Finite-data error.** The matched standard learner is a tabular action-conditional predictive state. It estimates the vector of next-position probabilities for the probe from repeated training histories. Use fixed symmetric Dirichlet pseudocount 0.5 per category and the same decoder in each arm. The full history vector is expressive for this finite alphabet. Compare its fitted risk with the full-history oracle for that arm. Any persistent misspecification or estimator-objective gap is reported separately from finite-sample error.
- **Compression-induced loss.** Apply the same fitting rule to Z, retaining S exactly where available. Report R*(Z)−R*(H) under a common sensor contract and also the fitted difference. The decomposition for the compressed fit is R_fit(Z)−R*(H) = [R*(Z)−R*(H)] + [R_fit(Z)−R*(Z)]. The fitted compressed-minus-full contrast also contains the difference in finite-data errors. Report oracle decoding before comparing fitted decoders, including any within-resolution difference between the empirical conditional mixture and a TV-optimal action.

The primary empirical contrast is the paired compressed-minus-full fitted TV risk in the sensor arm at 768 training episodes. The predeclared reference compression gap is 0.35 TV. Secondary contrasts are the raw-history oracle improvement due to S, the other sample sizes, full and compressed estimation gaps, and base-arm performance. A model recovering the standard action-conditional prediction already solves the part measurable from its permitted history.

## Execution design for a future freeze

Use 200 independent blocks. Each block has mutually independent training episodes and 512 new scoring episodes. Within a block use nested training sizes 48, 192 and 768. Both resolutions and both sensor arms share exactly the same training episodes, one realized probe label per training episode and the same scoring episodes. Context is drawn independently for every episode, including training episodes. Training interventions reveal population response statistics, while a private test context remains independent of every training episode. Restrict the learner to observable histories, arm-specific sensor values and training actions/targets. Do not feed score-known response laws, passive velocity computed outside the allowed history, or test outcomes to any learned method. There is no hyperparameter tuning in the draft. Generate split seed streams only after protocol freeze.

Compute a two-sided 95% t interval on the paired block means for the primary contrast and report a fixed ±0.05 TV synthetic agreement margin around the 0.35 oracle gap as a decision aid. Report finite-data estimates and interval, target coverage and any zero-count cell with its smoothing status. If matching the 0.35 reference is the inferential claim, require the full interval to fall in the margin. If the registered diagnostic recovers the three sources of error but offers no insight beyond the matched standard state predictor, describe it as an application and benchmark. Stop if label access differs, if identical passive laws fail the exact check, or if the fixed-sensor oracle fails to recover the endpoint. Resource plan is a local CPU run with finite count tables. Freeze code, environment, seeds, budgets and decision rules before requesting approval to execute. IC2 scoring is outside this proposal.
