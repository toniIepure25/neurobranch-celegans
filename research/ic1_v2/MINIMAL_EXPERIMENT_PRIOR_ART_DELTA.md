# Prior-art delta for the minimal experiment

| Precedent | Implication for the design |
|---|---|
| POMDP belief states and sufficient history | Exact filtering with supplied dynamics and a known prior is privileged. In the ring it gives the context uncertainty under H and resolves it with S. Exact filter performance cannot be used as a fair learned comparison. [Predictive state representations](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/hash/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Abstract.html) |
| Predictive states and controlled predictive-state models | Action-conditional predictions of future measurements are established. For the single probe and finite alphabet, the one-step tabular predictive-state vector is a direct matched standard. Hefny et al. treat richer controlled dynamical learning with two-stage regression. The draft uses the finite single-step specialization and claims no implementation of their full method. [Littman, Sutton and Singh](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/hash/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Abstract.html) · [Hefny, Downey and Gordon](https://cdn.aaai.org/ojs/11773/11773-13-15301-1-2-20201228.pdf) |
| Causal consistency and approximate abstraction | Cross-level agreement under mapped interventions already has formal definitions. Here the response stays fixed externally, with explicit passive support and a known test law to quantify distinct sources of error. [Exact transformations](https://proceedings.mlr.press/r15/rubenstein-17a.html) · [Approximate causal abstraction](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v115/beckers20a.html) |
| Partial identification and experiment design | Observationally equivalent mechanisms can induce a set of target laws, and experimental acquisition changes that set. This draft fixes the single measurement prospectively. It evaluates the consequence of the sensor under a TV loss without optimizing measurement selection. [Targeted causal experiment design](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/675e371eeeea99551ce47797ed6ed33e-Paper-Conference.pdf) |

The question still open is whether this particular calibrated neural-dynamical benchmark yields a useful diagnostic over the strongest matched predictive-state reference. The model pair, inequality and passive sensor effect are explicit controls. If the standard reference accounts for every observed pattern, preserve that as a negative methodological result.
