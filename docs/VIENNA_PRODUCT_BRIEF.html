Vienna Neuroinformatics Studio

A three-tier, source-traceable simulator stack for C. elegans whole-brain data
Prepared for: Denis Kovačević, Research Group Neuroinformatics, University of Vienna  ·  Prepared by: NeuroBranch  ·  Date: 9 November 2026  ·  Build: 9c8bdf6
What this is. A working research product that runs the group's published models and makes each model's evidential status explicit. It is deliberately not one overclaimed “digital twin”. What it is not. It does not establish whole-brain mechanism, a mechanistic simulation, or experimentally identified causality.

3. What is deliberately not claimed

  • Not a digital twin. A digital twin requires a validated, individually calibrated, causally predictive replica. None of the three tiers meets that bar.
  • Tier 1 is not a circuit model. The 3-D latent is a behaviourally relevant compression; it does not contain identified neurons or synapses.
  • Tier 2 is not an identified causal effect. The intervention panel propagates a forced state through a fitted Markov matrix. Real do() claims need experimental perturbation and the NC-MCM assumptions.
  • Tier 3 is not the published simulation. The paper simulates about 15 intrinsic neurons and clamps 69 signal neurons to recordings. The product runs all 205 nodes freely; that free-run becomes unstable at the published β = 10, so the default is β = 0 with an explicit warning and stability scan.
  • BunDLe-Net is a preprint. Cite it as bioRxiv 10.1101/2023.08.08.551978, not as a peer-reviewed journal article.
  • Five worms. Worm-level splits are correct, but n = 5 limits generalization claims.

4. How to use it

Live product

The current build is served from the H100 pod and exposed through an SSH tunnel:

http://localhost:8504

The tunnel must remain open. The app is a Streamlit research UI, not a public web service.

Reproduce from source

python scripts/fetch_vienna_artifacts.py
python -m streamlit run src/neurobranch/bundlenet_sim/app.py

Third-party artifacts are restored and SHA-256 checked; they are not committed because their licenses differ.

Suggested first meeting flow

  • Overview — agree on the claim boundaries before looking at numbers.
  • Tier 1 — compare manifold walk, switching linear, and quadratic on the same held-out worm.
  • Tier 2 — pick K, inspect the Markov p-value and transition uncertainty, then run a forced-state trajectory.
  • Tier 3 — run the chemical-only ODE, then use the β scan to see where the free-run leaves the stable regime.
  • Evidence report — download the Markdown report; every number and caveat travels with it.
